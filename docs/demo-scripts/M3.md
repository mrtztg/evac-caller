# M3 demo script: map dashboard

The dashboard shows the same replay as `pnpm replay` and the same calls as Telegram. Design: `docs/mockups/b-map-first.html` (chosen at the M3 UI gate).

Two processes: `pnpm web` (dashboard, port 3000) and `pnpm bot` (Telegram). They talk through two files in `data/runtime/` (git-ignored): `call-events.jsonl` (what happened on the phone line) and `replay-time.txt` (the hour the slider is on).

## Steps

1. `scripts/check.sh`. Expect `check: OK`.

2. Start fresh: `rm -rf data/runtime`. Then `pnpm web`. Expect `Ready in ...` and no error lines.

3. The dashboard uses the same numbers as the command line. Run both and compare:
   - `curl -s http://localhost:3000/api/incident | python3 -c "import json,sys; d=json.load(sys.stdin); print(d['time'], len(d['front']), d['counts'], [p['name'] for p in d['places'][:3]])"`
   - Expect exactly:
     `2026-07-25T14:00:00.000Z 197 {'within_1h': 26, 'within_3h': 67, 'within_6h': 158} ["Centre de Salut la Vall d'Uixó 2", "Centre de Salut de la Vall d'Uixó I", 'Centre de Salut Nules']`
   - `pnpm replay 3` must print the same hotspot count, the same counts and the same first three places for `2026-07-25 14:00 UTC`.

4. Open http://localhost:3000. Expect, with no placeholder text anywhere:
   - A full-screen dark map with the real fire hotspots (orange dots), three danger zones (1 h, 3 h, 6 h) as a cone pointing away from the wind, and a coloured dot for every place at risk.
   - Top left badge: `REPLAY — real data from a past fire (25 Jul 2026)`. Top right badge: `LIVE CALLS via SLNG`.
   - Incident card: `La Vall d'Uixó wildfire, Castellón (July 2026)`, `Replay hour 16:00 Spanish time`, 197 hotspots, satellite pass `13:33 UTC`, wind `from the west at 41 km/h`, spread `4.1 km/h`, and the three pills `26 / 67 / 158`.
   - The card says arrival times are an estimate from a wind cone, not a fire simulation, and names the wind station.
   - Places card: `158 in the next 6 h`, ranked, the first three as in step 3, with distance, fire direction and arrival estimate. Schools and nurseries show the grey tag `likely empty: weekend`.
   - Calls card: `No calls yet.` (nothing has been approved yet).

5. Click a place in the list. Its dot on the map grows. Click a dot on the map: the same place is selected in the list.

6. Move the replay slider to `2026-07-25 12:00 UTC` (the fourth bar). Expect the map, the counts and the list to change (27 hotspots at that hour), and:
   - `cat data/runtime/replay-time.txt` shows `2026-07-25T12:00:00.000Z`.

7. The bot follows the slider. With the slider still on 12:00 UTC, run `pnpm bot` and ask in Telegram "How many places are at risk?". Expect the counts for 12:00 UTC (`≤1h 26, ≤3h 41, ≤6h 52`), not the counts for 16:00. Move the slider back to `14:00 UTC` before the demo.

8. **NEEDS HUMAN (phone):** in Telegram send "Call the three health centres", press **Approve**. Expect, within about 2 seconds, on the dashboard:
   - A card under **Calls** for the first place with status `IN CALL`, the call ID, `approved by <your name>` and the dispatch latency in ms.
   - When the call ends: the status changes to `CONFIRMED`, `NEEDS HELP`, `NO ANSWER` or `UNCLEAR`, with the caller's quote, the transcript, the call duration, the voice latency numbers (e2e, LLM, TTS) and the classifier model, latency and tokens.
   - The same result appears in Telegram. The two must agree.

9. Safety check: no call may start without an approval. Press **Deny** instead of Approve on a new request. Expect no new card under **Calls** and no new line in `data/runtime/call-events.jsonl`.

10. In Telegram, press **Escalated to 112** on an unconfirmed place. Expect the dashboard card to show `<name> escalated this place to 112`.

## What the judge sees
The map replays the real fire, the list is ranked by how soon the fire can arrive, a human approves in Telegram, the phone rings, and the result with real numbers lands on the big screen.
